> [!WARNING]
> Cybersecurity frameworks are not specific tools or methodologies.

They are a structured set of standards, guidelines, and best practices designed to help organizations manage and reduce cyber-security risks. These frameworks provide a comprehensive roadmap for assessing, monitoring, and mitigating potential threats.

> [!TIP]
> Those frameworks are critical for aligning security efforts across different teams, industries, and countries. They enable security leaders -- such as CISO's, risk management teams, and IT leadership - to effectively assess their own security posture as well as that of third-party vendors, ensuring a unified approach to threat mitigation.

These framework form the backbone of an organization's cybersecurity strategy.

## 7 of the most widely used frameworks and standards

# 1. NIST 2.0 Framework

The NIST Cyber security Framework (NIST CSF) provides a multi-step process to implement what is known as [best practices when protecting our assets](https://www.armis.com/solution-briefs/alignment-to-nist-csf/) and infrastructure.

NIST has detailed 5 critical functions that need adherence:

    Identify
    Protect
    Detect
    Respond
    Recover
