## Key Terms

Being familiar with the terminology used can help that person better identify the threats that can harm organizations and people alike. A security analyst or cybersecurity analyst focuses on **monitoring networks** for **breaches**.

They also aid in the development of strategies to secure an organization and research information technology security trends to remain alert and informed about potential threats.

Additionally, an **analyst** works to prevent incidents.
